I work at the intersection of **industrial automation, applied machine learning, and edge AI**. I founded **QAVEAI** to help engineering teams turn equipment data and plant knowledge into practical troubleshooting and maintenance decisions.

## Building at QAVEAI

We are developing a **private industrial AI copilot** that brings machine signals, manuals, SOPs, and maintenance records into one source-grounded workflow for technicians and engineers.

- **Local intelligence** — on-premise inference and retrieval designed around plant constraints.
- **Industrial context** — connect equipment signals with the documentation needed to interpret them.
- **Engineer-led decisions** — present evidence, uncertainty, and checks for human review.
- **Measured value** — start with one asset and one recurring failure mode, then evaluate against a baseline.

The copilot is in development. The public demos use fictional data and scripted scenarios to illustrate the proposed workflow.
